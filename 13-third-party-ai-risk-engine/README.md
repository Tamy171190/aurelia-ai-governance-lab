# Project 13: Third Party AI Risk Engine

## Purpose

A vendor governance workflow for external AI models, platforms and specialist AI providers used by Aurelia Financial Group.

## Core flow

Vendor identification -> criticality -> due diligence -> contractual controls -> technical evidence -> jurisdictional mapping -> ongoing monitoring -> exit readiness

## Financial services scenarios

- External research AI
- Valuation models and fine-tuned models
- KYC and AML providers
- Generative AI platforms
- NAV reconciliation services

## Key questions

- What AI capability is being provided?
- Who is the provider and what role do they perform?
- What data is processed and where?
- Can the provider train on Aurelia data?
- How are model changes notified?
- What technical and governance evidence is available?
- What incident notification and audit rights exist?
- Can Aurelia validate performance independently?
- What happens if the provider becomes unavailable or non-compliant?

## Multi-jurisdiction design

The engine maps vendor risks across EU, UK, Singapore and UAE requirements and uses ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF as cross-framework governance baselines.

## Note

This is a fictional learning and demonstration environment. It is not legal, regulatory or compliance advice.
