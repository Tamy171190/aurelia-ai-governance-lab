# Project 10 — AI Governance Audit

Perform an independent audit of Aurelia's AI Governance Management System (AIMS).

## Audit objectives

The audit will cover:

- Governance design
- AI inventory completeness
- Risk classification and applicability assessment
- Multi-jurisdiction regulatory mapping
- Control design
- Evidence completeness
- Operating effectiveness
- Monitoring
- Incident management
- Vendor governance
- Remediation

## Jurisdictional audit lens

For each sampled AI system, the auditor should determine whether Aurelia:

1. Identified the relevant operating and processing geographies.
2. Assessed EU, UK, Singapore and UAE applicability rather than assuming it.
3. Mapped applicable requirements to the control framework.
4. Applied the highest applicable control requirement where appropriate.
5. Documented jurisdiction-specific enhancements and exceptions.
6. Maintained evidence supporting the control conclusion.

## Assurance structure

Findings will use:

**Condition → Criteria / Requirement → Risk → Root Cause → Recommendation → Management Response → Priority → Evidence Required → Closure Test**

The audit should distinguish between:

- Regulatory non-compliance
- Framework alignment gap
- Internal policy/control gap
- Evidence deficiency
- Design effectiveness issue
- Operating effectiveness issue
- Applicability uncertainty requiring legal or regulatory review

This distinction is important because a framework gap is not automatically a legal breach.
