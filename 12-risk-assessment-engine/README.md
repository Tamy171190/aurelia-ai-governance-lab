# Project 12: AI Risk Assessment Engine

## Purpose

A practical risk assessment workflow for Aurelia Financial Group. The engine converts AI inventory information into inherent risk, control effectiveness and residual risk assessments.

## Core flow

AI system -> risk -> likelihood -> impact -> inherent risk -> control effectiveness -> residual risk -> treatment

## Financial services scenarios

- Investment research
- Portfolio valuation support
- NAV reconciliation
- Investor communications
- KYC and AML monitoring
- Client-facing AI

## Multi-jurisdiction design

The assessment is designed to support EU, UK, Singapore and UAE applicability while using ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF as cross-framework governance baselines.

The control ceiling principle is used where multiple applicable frameworks address the same risk. This is a design approach, not a statement that the strictest regulation automatically applies in every jurisdiction.

## Evidence

The engine expects documented risk rationale, control effectiveness evidence, residual risk approval and treatment decisions.

## Note

This is a fictional learning and demonstration environment. It is not legal, regulatory or compliance advice.
