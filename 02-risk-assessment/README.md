# Project 02 - AI Risk Assessment Framework

This project will extend the inventory into a structured AI risk assessment process.

## Planned workflow

AI system -> risk identification -> inherent risk -> control assessment -> residual risk -> treatment -> owner -> monitoring

## Risk domains

- Governance and accountability
- Data quality and privacy
- Model performance and robustness
- Bias and fairness
- Explainability and transparency
- Human oversight
- Cybersecurity and misuse
- Third-party and supply-chain risk
- Regulatory and contractual exposure
- Financial and reputational impact

The assessment will use the inventory created in Project 01 and will explicitly distinguish illustrative methodology from actual legal or regulatory requirements.
