# Project 11 - Board AI Governance Dashboard

Translate the AI inventory, risk, control and evidence information into concise Board and senior-management reporting.

## Executive dashboard design

The dashboard is designed to answer six questions quickly:

1. What AI systems do we have?
2. How many systems sit in high, moderate or lower governance tiers?
3. Where are the material inherent and residual risks?
4. Are controls working and is evidence complete?
5. How well are the AIMS controls covering the UK, EU, Singapore and UAE requirements, plus ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF?
6. What requires management or Board attention?

## Core KPI cards

- AI systems in inventory
- High governance tier systems
- Moderate governance tier systems
- Third-party AI dependencies
- Evidence completeness
- Open or partial evidence items
- Control effectiveness

## Risk view

The dashboard should show the distribution of high, moderate and low risks and distinguish inherent risk from residual risk where data permits.

## Multi-jurisdiction coverage

Coverage is shown across:

- European Union
- United Kingdom
- Singapore
- United Arab Emirates
- ISO/IEC 42001
- NIST AI RMF

Coverage should be calculated from the underlying applicability and control records. It should not imply that every AI system is subject to every framework.

## Control ceiling indicator

The dashboard should show whether the highest applicable control requirement has been identified and translated into an Aurelia control. Exceptions should be visible where a jurisdiction-specific enhancement is required.

## Governance indicators

Recommended indicators include:

- AI inventory coverage
- High-tier risk assessment coverage
- Evidence completeness
- Control effectiveness
- Vendor due diligence completion
- Human oversight effectiveness
- Monitoring readiness
- Overdue remediation

## Priority actions

The dashboard should surface the most important management actions, such as vendor remediation, post-deployment monitoring, bias and performance testing, human oversight testing and jurisdiction-specific control enhancements.

## Design principle

The dashboard is the executive output of the AIMS. It should link back to the underlying inventory, risk register, control library, evidence pack and regulatory mapping rather than relying on manually entered headline numbers.
