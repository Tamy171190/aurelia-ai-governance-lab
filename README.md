# Aurelia AI Governance Lab

A practical AI Governance and Assurance portfolio built around a fictional global financial-services group, Aurelia Capital Group.

## Purpose

This lab demonstrates how AI Governance principles can be translated into practical processes, controls, evidence, risk assessments and assurance activities across financial services.

The portfolio draws on concepts from ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and other relevant governance frameworks. Regulatory requirements will be clearly distinguished from illustrative methodology and design choices used in this fictional environment.

## Aurelia Capital Group

A fictional UK/EU-headquartered financial-services group with approximately €25–30bn AUM and operations across:

- United Kingdom
- European Union
- Singapore
- United Arab Emirates

Business areas include private equity, asset management, fund administration, investment research, investor relations, finance, risk & compliance, HR and technology.

## AI Governance Lifecycle

**Discover → Inventory → Classify → Assess → Govern → Control → Implement → Monitor → Assure → Report**

## Portfolio Roadmap

1. **AI Systems Inventory & Classification Engine** — identify AI systems, capture governance attributes and apply an illustrative risk-classification methodology.
2. **AI Risk Assessment Engine** — assess inherent and residual AI risks and define treatment requirements.
3. **AI Governance Framework** — establish governance principles, roles, decision rights and oversight.
4. **AI Control Framework** — map governance requirements to controls and evidence.
5. **ISO/IEC 42001 Alignment** — demonstrate how an AI management system can be structured against the standard.
6. **EU AI Act Assessment** — create a fictional regulatory-readiness and conformity assessment workflow.
7. **AI Incident Management** — simulate detection, triage, escalation, remediation and reporting.
8. **Third-Party AI Risk** — assess external foundation models and AI vendors.
9. **AI Conformity Assessment** — develop evidence-based assessment for selected high-risk use cases.
10. **AI Governance Audit** — independently test governance design and operating effectiveness.
11. **Board AI Governance Dashboard** — translate AI risk information into senior-management reporting.
12. **AI Governance Stress Tests** — deliberately challenge the framework using realistic financial-services scenarios.

## Design Principles

- Financial-services context
- Evidence-based governance
- Risk-based classification
- Human oversight
- Accountability and traceability
- Regulatory awareness
- Practical implementation
- Independent assurance

## Important Note

This is a fictional learning and demonstration environment. It does not represent Aurelia Capital Group or any real organisation, and it is not legal, regulatory or compliance advice.
